Section 1 Featured Image Shows how much time and money it takes to convert a van into a home. The image appears between the section heading and first paragraph, with its caption underneath. I will keep the image in normal flow and use CSS clip-path to create a rounded arch, representing a doorway into the van-life lifestyle while emphasizing the finished interior. The image stays in normal flow inside its figure. The clip-path creates a stacking context, but no additional z-index is needed. If clip-path is not supported or is disabled, the image will display with rounded corners using border-radius. Disable clip-path in DevTools and verify that the image remains visible with rounded corners.
Header Badge (.issue-badge) Highlights the financial realities and hidden costs associated with van-life. Without absolute positioning, the badge appears beneath the introduction in normal document flow. I used position: relative on the feature header and position: absolute on the badge to anchor it to the upper-right corner. This creates a purposeful relationship between the badge and the story header. The feature header (.feature-header) is the containing block for the absolutely positioned badge. No additional z-index or stacking context is needed. At viewport widths of 600px or less, position: static returns the badge to normal flow, preventing it from overlapping the heading or introduction. Test at 320px, 768px, and 1280px. Verify that the badge remains within the header, does not cover text, and moves beneath the introduction on smaller screens.
Sample Budget Field Note (.budget-label) Identifies the monthly budget as a practical financial reference for aspiring van-lifers. The field-note label appears before the budget heading in normal document flow. I used position: relative on the budget card and position: absolute on the field-note label to create a controlled overlap along the card's upper border. The budget card (.budget-card) establishes the containing block. The label uses z-index: var(--layer-detail) to create a small, predictable stacking layer above the card border. No arbitrary high z-index values are needed. The label remains attached to the budget card at different viewport widths. If absolute positioning is disabled, the label returns to normal flow. Test at 320px, 768px, and 1280px. Confirm the label remains readable, does not cover content, and scrolls with the budget card.
Story Navigation (.story-guide) Helps readers move between the two sections of The Price of Freedom without scrolling through the entire article. The navigation appears before the article in normal document flow, with links to both sections. I used position: sticky to keep the navigation accessible while scrolling on larger screens. CSS Grid creates a separate navigation column. The navigation is a Grid item inside .story-layout. The story-layout container establishes the boundary for sticky positioning, preventing the navigation from continuing into the documentation section. Sticky positioning creates a stacking context, and no explicit z-index is required. Below 52rem, the navigation returns to normal flow and scrolls with the page, preserving reading space on smaller screens. Test at 320px, 768px, and 1280px. Verify sticky behavior at 1280px, normal scrolling on mobile, section boundaries, and keyboard access to both links.
Campsite Photograph (.campsite-figure) Illustrates the van-life experience while supporting the discussion of ongoing travel and camping expenses. The campsite photograph and photographer credit remain together in a figure before the introductory paragraphs. I used border-radius: 50% to create a circular image. On larger screens, float and shape-outside: circle(50%) allow the paragraphs to wrap around the photograph. Shape-margin adds spacing between the image and text. The figure participates in normal document flow. On desktop, it floats within the article section. No additional z-index or stacking context is required. If shape-outside is unsupported or disabled, text wraps around the figure's rectangular float boundary. Below 52rem, the figure returns to normal flow above the paragraphs. Test at 320px, 768px, and 1280px. Disable shape-outside to check readability. Verify the circular crop, image credit, text spacing, and mobile layout.
